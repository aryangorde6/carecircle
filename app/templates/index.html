Medicines and doctor visits, managed together.

One shared, passcode-protected care profile for an elder, their caretakers and family. Gentle reminders first, then the family is told only if a dose is really missed. The elder decides who can see and change what.

One click. A private circle with a week of sample data, no sign-up.

When a dose is not marked

  1. 15 minutes before

    The elder gets a gentle reminder.

  2. 30 minutes late

    Caretakers are emailed too.

  3. 60 minutes late

    Family is asked to check in.

Marking the dose as taken or skipped stops the reminders.

What makes it different

  • 📷

    Photo to schedule

    Snap a prescription; Gemini drafts the medicine list for you to check.

  • 🔑

    The elder is in charge

    They choose who can only view and who can edit, and can change it any time.

  • 🙂

    The elder's own voice

    A one-tap "How are you feeling?" each day. The family is emailed about a bad day only if the elder chooses.

  • 📝

    Weekly update in their language

    Gemini writes a plain summary and questions for the doctor, in English, Hindi, Tamil and more.

  • 💊

    Never run out

    Doses left count down as they are taken; the circle gets an email before the strip is empty.

  • 🩺

    Ready for the doctor

    A printable visit sheet with medicines, adherence, how they have felt and what to ask.
